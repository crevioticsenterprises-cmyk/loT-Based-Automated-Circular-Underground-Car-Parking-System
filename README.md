# 📖 Project Overview

The **AI-Based Automated Circular Underground Parking System** is an innovative smart parking solution designed to maximize parking capacity while minimizing land usage.

The proposed system consists of **25 underground circular parking layers**, each containing **8 parking slots**, allowing a total capacity of **200 vehicles** in a compact underground structure.

The system integrates **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud Computing, Computer Vision, QR/RFID Authentication, Hydraulic Automation, and Smart Security** to automate parking, improve security, reduce waiting time, and support future smart city infrastructure.

---

# ❗ Problem Statement

Modern cities face serious parking challenges including:

- Lack of parking space
- Traffic congestion
- Time wasted searching for parking
- Poor land utilization
- Vehicle theft
- Manual parking management
- High carbon emissions
- Lack of real-time parking information

Traditional parking systems occupy large surface areas and are inefficient for growing urban environments.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

Our project introduces an **Automated Circular Underground Parking System** capable of storing **200 vehicles** using a multi-layer underground rotating parking mechanism.

The system automatically:

- Detects vehicle entry
- Finds available parking slots
- Assigns parking location
- Generates QR Code / RFID
- Captures vehicle images
- Stores parking information in Cloud
- Retrieves vehicles automatically
- Calculates parking fee
- Updates parking availability in real time

# ⚙ System Workflow

## Vehicle Parking

1. Vehicle arrives.
2. Ultrasonic sensor detects vehicle.
3. Camera captures vehicle image.
4. IR sensors identify empty slot.
5. QR/RFID generated.
6. Vehicle information stored in cloud.
7. Hydraulic lift moves required layer.
8. Parking rack rotates.
9. Vehicle parked successfully.
10. LCD updates available parking slots.

---

## Vehicle Retrieval

1. User scans QR Code or RFID.
2. Parking location identified.
3. Hydraulic lift moves selected layer.
4. Rack rotates.
5. Camera verifies vehicle.
6. Parking fee calculated.
7. Payment completed.
8. Servo gate opens.
9. LCD updates parking count.
